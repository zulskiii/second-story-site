To help us keep every dress beautiful for the next wearer, please handle your rental with care.

Before Wearing

  • Wear deodorant or antiperspirant
  • Wear on clean, dry skin
  • Avoid excessive perfume, body oil, self-tanner, and makeup transfer on fabric

While Wearing

  • Handle with care
  • Be mindful of makeup, drinks, and food
  • Avoid rough surfaces that may snag delicate fabric

Please Don't

  • Wash the dress
  • Iron or steam the dress
  • Sew, cut, pin, or alter the garment
  • Use fabric tape, glue, or adhesives

After Use

  • Return the dress on time
  • Return all included items and accessories
  • Inform us immediately if accidental stains or issues happen
Normal wear is understandable. However, excessive stains — especially heavy underarm sweat stains, odor retention, makeup transfer, spills, or irreversible discoloration — may incur cleaning charges or deductions from the security deposit depending on severity. White and light-colored dresses are especially delicate and prone to permanent staining, so we kindly ask for extra care while wearing them.
